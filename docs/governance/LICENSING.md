# AgentKern Licensing & Business Model (2026)

AgentKern uses an **Open Core** model designed for massive adoption and enterprise stability. We give away the "Ghost" (the engine) to define the standard, and sell the "Mantle" (the safety layer) for enterprise orchestration.

---

## 🏛️ The Open Core Model

| Tier | License | Focus |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Community** | Apache 2.0 | Core pillars, basic protocols, single-node safety. |
| **Enterprise** | Commercial | Multi-node mesh, global sync, advanced compliance. |

### 1. The Open Zone (Apache 2.0)
These components are free forever to ensure no vendor lock-in and rapid developer adoption.
- **Core Pillars**: Identity, Gate, Synapse, Arbiter, Nexus.
- **SDKs**: TypeScript, Python, and Rust core logic.
- **Protocols**: A2A, MCP, and native AgentKern framing.

### 2. The Commercial Zone (Enterprise)
Managed features for production swarms at scale.
- **Global Mesh Sync**: Multi-region CRDT synchronization (`ee/sovereign-mesh`).
- **Managed Multi-tenancy**: Hard resource isolation and quotas (`ee/multitenancy`).
- **Managed Treasury**: Institutional-grade wallets and insurance (`ee/treasury`).
- **Advanced Compliance**: Automated SOC2/ISO 27001 evidence export.
